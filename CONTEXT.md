# Cultural Atlas

Cultural Atlas helps visitors understand cities through concise discoveries across history, art, and culture. This document defines the domain vocabulary and core rules; see the [product vision](docs/product/vision.md) for product goals.

## Publication

A POI appears on the visitor map only when it has:

- valid versioned **Story Content** generated by the **Story Workflow**;
- a selected **Main Image** with source, author or rights status, license, and attribution;
- saved **Person** records for every retained **Related Person**.

A Story may have no Related People. Imported places and metadata-only POIs remain internal. Curators may edit generated content; updates reach the public site through deployment.

Explicit approval is a possible future evolution, not a current requirement or a prerequisite for new discovery paths. Draft and published content currently share the same stored structure without separate approval states. If approval is introduced, it would cover both the Story Content and selected Main Image.

## Language

**POI**:
An app-ready place or object on the map that may have metadata and a story.
_Avoid_: Clean POI, polished POI, transformed POI, spot, location, attraction

**Geo Place**:
The geographic representation of a place from a source dataset before irrelevant fields are removed and it becomes a **POI**.
_Avoid_: Raw POI, Raw Place, Raw Location, Raw feature, source feature

**POI ID**:
A stable, human-readable identifier assigned by Cultural Atlas to a **POI**, independent of identifiers from external sources.
_Avoid_: Wikidata ID, content slug

**POI Type**:
A Wikidata classification assigned to a **POI**, identified by a Wikidata item and readable label. It is source data that may inform **POI Categories**.
_Avoid_: P31, category

**POI Category**:
An app-owned grouping shared across cities, with a singular name, used to filter **POIs** by their current or historical nature or function in the **Visitor Experience**. A **POI** may belong to several categories, including categories assigned directly by a **Curator** without **POI Types**; category membership does not imply current use or visitor access.
_Avoid_: POI Type, Wikidata category

**POI Subcategory**:
A **POI Category** nested directly under a broader parent **POI Category**. Its **POIs** also belong to the parent's discovery scope; Cathedral, Basilica, and Church are subcategories of Churches & cathedrals. Discovery scopes within that group are disjoint and follow the place naming rules.
_Avoid_: POI Type, Wikidata subclass

**POI Type Mapping**:
A shared editorial rule assigning a **POI Type** to zero or more **POI Categories** across cities. An explicitly ignored type is distinct from a type that has not yet been mapped.
_Avoid_: Wikidata category, city category mapping

**POI Category Exception**:
A Curator's explicit addition or exclusion of a **POI Category** for one **POI**, relative to the shared **POI Type Mapping**. It remains an editorial decision until the Curator removes it, independently of changes to source types or shared mappings.
_Avoid_: Local type mapping, source correction

**Story Workflow**:
The editorial process combining automated **Draft Story Generation** and human **Story Curation** for a **POI**.
_Avoid_: Draft Workflow, production workflow, admin workflow

**Draft Story Generation**:
The automated part of the **Story Workflow** that gathers **Sources** and creates or updates a **Draft Story**, its **Main Image Candidates**, and its **Draft Main Image**.
_Avoid_: Generation Pipeline, AI pipeline, draft workflow

**Story Curation**:
Human review, editing, and enrichment of a **Draft Story**, including its **Draft Main Image**.
_Avoid_: Review Workflow, approval workflow, manual workflow

**Curator**:
The human decision-maker responsible for **Story Curation**.
_Avoid_: Admin, editor, reviewer

**POI Metadata**:
Basic identifying information for a **POI**, such as name, location, city, period, Wikidata ID, and short descriptor.
_Avoid_: Visitor narrative, curated content, story metadata

**Story**:
The source-grounded visitor-facing explanation for one **POI**, including **Story Content** and a main image.
_Avoid_: POI Story, Visitor Narrative, article, summary, description, AI text, Markdown

**Draft Story**:
A source-grounded story for one **POI** being prepared or reviewed editorially.
_Avoid_: Draft POI Story, POI Draft, draft narrative, AI Markdown, AI text, Markdown, generated output

**Story Content**:
The structured, source-grounded content that supplies the **Visitor Experience**, composed of one **Story Introduction**, optional **Story Topics**, and **Related People**.
_Avoid_: Story body, Markdown, MDX, rendered page

**Story Introduction**:
The required plain-text opening of **Story Content** that gives the visitor a concise orientation to the **POI**.
_Avoid_: Summary, title, lead Markdown

**Story Topic**:
A history, design, or art grouping in **Story Content**. Topics contain draft cultural content or published **Insights**, and may be empty when unsupported by **Sources**.
_Avoid_: Required section, content slot, card

**Visitor Insight**:
A concise cultural idea shown to the visitor, helping them notice, understand, connect, remember, or navigate a **POI**. **Insight** means the same thing. Unpublished proposals are draft content; internal rationale is **Editorial Notes**.
_Avoid_: Fact, section, card, reasoning, internal rationale, unpublished proposal

**Editorial Notes**:
Internal rationale, selection explanations, review comments, and generation notes. They are not **Insights** and remain hidden from the default **Visitor Experience**.
_Avoid_: Insight, Visitor Insight

**Person**:
A historical, mythological, or imaginary individual represented once in Cultural Atlas, with an internal ID, external identity, source-grounded description, and optional curiosities, dates, and image.
_Avoid_: Person Profile, Cultural Figure, character, biography article

**Related Person**:
A person reference in **Story Content**, selected for its significance to the **POI**. Resolved references link to the canonical **Person**.
_Avoid_: Mention, character, unreferenced biography

**Unresolved Person Reference**:
A person reference found during **Draft Story Generation** whose identity cannot yet be matched safely to a **Person ID**.
_Avoid_: Person, guessed identity, duplicate Person

**Main Image**:
The image in a story that helps visitors recognize a **POI** or notice an important visible detail.
_Avoid_: Lead Image Candidate, decoration, gallery image

**Draft Main Image**:
The image selected for a **Draft Story**, used as the visitor-facing **Main Image** when publication requirements are met.
_Avoid_: Proposed Main Image, selected image, temporary Main Image

**Main Image Candidate**:
A workflow-only image option from which **Draft Story Generation** or a **Curator** selects the **Draft Main Image**.
_Avoid_: Alternative, gallery image, image result

**Source**:
External cultural material used to ground or review a draft story.
_Avoid_: Source Material, claim reference, citation

**Source Reference**:
An internal reference from a **Story Introduction**, **Visitor Insight**, or **Related Person** to a persisted **Source**. It supports curator review and is omitted from the default **Visitor Experience**.
_Avoid_: public footnote, URL embedded in prose, bibliography entry

**Visitor Experience**:
The public-facing experience that shows publishable stories from the public catalog to visitors.
_Avoid_: Production output, AI output

## Core rules

### Places and categories

- A **Geo Place** becomes an app-ready **POI** before the **Story Workflow** starts. Each POI has one stable app-owned ID and optional external identifiers.
- A POI may have several source **POI Types** and app-owned **POI Categories**. Metadata and category membership remain separate from Story Content. Category exceptions survive source and mapping changes until the Curator removes them.
- Category hierarchy, naming, and filtering rules are defined in [POI Categories](docs/poi-categories.md).

### Stories and workflow

- A POI has at most one current Draft Story and one visitor-facing Story, each belonging to that POI. A Story combines Story Content and one Main Image.
- Draft Story Generation starts from an existing POI and creates or replaces draft content, Sources, and image candidates. Regenerating content or candidates remains automated generation; reviewing content and selecting an image are Story Curation.
- A Draft Story contains Story Content and one or more Sources; its selected image and candidates may be missing while generation is incomplete.
- Story Content is plain text with a required introduction, optional supported history/design/art topics, and Related People. Topics express content semantics; React components control presentation and need not expose one section per topic.
- Publish only the strongest few source-supported ideas rather than a complete article summary. Prefer visible details when they convey the cultural meaning. Dated history insights run oldest to newest, followed by undated insights.
- Retain Sources and resolvable Source References for editorial review. Sources, Editorial Notes, AI reasoning, and processing metadata remain hidden from the default Visitor Experience.
- Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Wikimedia Commons provide the initial source material; story text currently starts from Wikipedia. MDX and configurable React renderers remain future decisions.
- Generation order, persistence, retries, and failure behavior are defined in [Story Workflow Architecture](docs/story-workflow-architecture.md).

### People

- Select at most ten Related People significant to understanding the POI, ordered from most to least significant.
- A Person is canonical and reused across Stories, with one app-owned ID and a retained Wikidata identity. It requires a resolved identity, name, source-grounded description, and its own Sources, separate from Story Sources.
- Curiosities, birth/death dates, and at most one image are optional. Dates may be exact or approximate and omitted when unknown or inapplicable; curiosities must be source-supported.
- Wikipedia supplies Person content, Wikidata canonical identity, and Commons image and rights metadata. Successfully generated People are available in the editorial workflow without separate approval.
- Ambiguous identities remain unresolved rather than guessed. A Person failure preserves Story Content for review and retry without regenerating it, but blocks the POI's publication under the rules above.
- Removing a Related Person removes only the Story reference, not the canonical Person. Every published Related Person links to a saved record.
- Resolution and navigation details are defined in [Person Architecture](docs/person-architecture.md).

### Images

- Draft Story Generation proposes up to three Wikimedia Commons candidates that help recognize the POI. Wikipedia and Wikidata may help discover them; image source and rights metadata come from Commons.
- Preserve an existing selection when it remains an available candidate; otherwise select the first candidate with license and attribution. The Curator may change it during review.
- Candidates remain editorial artifacts. Only the selected Main Image appears in the Story and may help recognize the place or notice a meaningful visible detail.
