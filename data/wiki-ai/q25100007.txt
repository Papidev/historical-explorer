The **Chiostro del Bramante** is a Renaissance cloister in Rome, built around 1500 by **Cardinal Oliviero Carafa** and designed by architect **Donato Bramante**. It originally served as part of a monastery complex.  

Today, the structure hosts **exhibitions, meetings, and concerts**. A **cafe** and **bookshop** operate within its walls.  

From the **first floor**, visitors can see **Raphael’s fresco** *The Sibyls*, located in the adjacent **Santa Maria della Pace** church. The artwork, completed in the 1510s, is a key example of High Renaissance art.  

The site reflects **Renaissance architectural innovation** and remains a functional space blending historical and modern uses.
